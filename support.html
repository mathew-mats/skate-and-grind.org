Why It Matters

SKATE AND GRIND is built on passion, but passion alone can't buy skateboards, helmets, or rent out safe spaces for our youth sessions. We operate on a shoe-string budget, relying entirely on the generosity of our community and supporters.

Every shilling goes directly back into the community. Your support helps us provide free equipment to beginners, host competitions that showcase local talent, and run outreach programs that use skateboarding to teach life skills to young people across Kampala.

What Your Support Does

Here's exactly how your contribution makes a tangible impact on the ground in Kampala.

Equipment & Safety

Funds go towards purchasing skateboards, helmets, knee pads, and other safety gear for young skaters who can't afford their own.

Events & Competitions

We organize local skate jams and competitions that give our community a platform to shine and bring skaters from all over Uganda together.

Outreach Programs

Your support allows us to run free outreach sessions in underserved communities, using skateboarding to promote mental health and life skills.

Our Transparency Promise

We believe in complete transparency. Every donation is used directly for the benefit of our programs and community. We do not pay salaries, we do not use funds for personal expenses, and we will always be open about how your money is being used. If you have any questions about our finances, please don't hesitate to reach out.
