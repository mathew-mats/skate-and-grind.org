Our Story

Born on the streets of Kampala, built by the community.

Who We Are

SKATE AND GRIND is not just a skateboarding club; we are a movement. Founded in the heart of Kampala, we started as a small group of friends sharing a passion for skateboarding and a desire to create a space where everyone felt welcome.

Over the years, we have grown into a vibrant community of skaters, artists, and dreamers. We believe that skateboarding is more than just a sport—it's a tool for self-expression, discipline, and social change. We are dedicated to providing a safe, inclusive environment where young people can learn, grow, and push their limits.

Our Mission

To empower the youth of Kampala through skateboarding, fostering a community built on respect, creativity, and resilience. We strive to provide the resources, mentorship, and safe spaces needed for every skater to reach their full potential, both on and off the board.

Our Vision

We envision a Kampala where skateboarding is celebrated as a positive force for youth development. A city where young people have the opportunities, confidence, and community support to turn their passions into purpose, and where the streets are a canvas for creativity and connection.

What Drives Us

Our core values shape everything we do, from the sessions we run to the community we build.

Community

We believe in the power of togetherness. We support each other, celebrate each other's wins, and lift each other up when we fall.

Resilience

Skateboarding teaches us to get back up after every fall. We apply that same grit and determination to every aspect of our lives.

Creativity

There's no right or wrong way to skate. We encourage our members to express themselves freely and find their own unique style.
