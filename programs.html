Our Programs

Building skills, confidence, and community through skateboarding.

What We Do

From beginner-friendly sessions to competitive street skating, our programs are designed to meet skaters where they are and help them grow. We use skateboarding as a tool to teach life skills, build resilience, and create a supportive community.

Youth Skate Sessions

Our flagship program provides a safe and encouraging space for young people to learn the fundamentals of skateboarding. We provide boards, helmets, and mentorship for beginners.

  • Weekly Saturday sessions
  • Free equipment loan
  • Focus on safety and basic tricks

Street Competitions

We organize and participate in local and national skate competitions. These events give our community a platform to showcase their skills, push their limits, and gain recognition.

  • Annual Kampala Skate Jam
  • Inter-community competitions
  • Progressive skill challenges

Community Outreach

Using skateboarding as a tool for social change, we run outreach programs in underserved areas of Kampala. We promote discipline, creativity, and positive mental health.

  • School and community visits
  • Life skills workshops
  • Environmental clean-up skate events

Girls in Skateboarding

We are committed to breaking barriers and creating a welcoming space for girls and young women to skate. This initiative focuses on empowerment, sisterhood, and visibility.

  • Dedicated girls-only sessions
  • Female mentorship program
  • Confidence-building workshops

Whether you want to learn, compete, or volunteer, there's a place for you at SKATE AND GRIND.
