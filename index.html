Who We Are

Born on the streets of Kampala, SKATE AND GRIND is more than just a skateboarding club. We are a movement dedicated to empowering the youth through the raw, unapologetic culture of skateboarding.

We provide a safe, creative space where skaters of all levels can grow, express themselves, and build lifelong connections. Whether you're landing your first ollie or mastering a kickflip, you have a home here.

What We Do

From grassroots youth sessions to city-wide competitions, our programs are designed to engage, educate, and elevate the next generation of Ugandan skaters.

Youth Sessions

Weekly meetups designed for beginners and young skaters to learn the basics, build confidence, and make friends in a supportive environment.

Street Competitions

We organize and participate in local and national skate competitions, giving our community a platform to showcase their skills and push their limits.

Community Outreach

Using skateboarding as a tool for social change, we run outreach programs that promote discipline, creativity, and positive mental health among Kampala's youth.
